Miżien

Weighing public claims against the evidence.

Miżien is Maltese for a scale or balance.

What this is

An open record of checks on public claims made in Malta by authorities, institutions, parties, NGOs and others, focused on quality of life and the environment. Each claim is tested against scientific sources first, then official data. Every source is archived so the record of what was said survives edits and deletions.

Science first

Peer-reviewed literature and independent datasets come before press releases.

Every side

Governments, opposition parties, NGOs and developers are held to the same standard.

Right of reply

Each draft goes to the body concerned before wider circulation, and its response is published.

Evidence, not inference

A verdict of Misleading or Contradicted requires documents or data that can be shown.

Verdict scale
