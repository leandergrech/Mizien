# CC-145 data

`wbru_2025_licences.csv`: the two tables on pp. 1-2 of the Wild Birds Regulation Unit's *Game Reporting Data – 2025
Report* (43 pp., July 2026), typed from the maintainer's browser copy (literature/CC-145, git-ignored; sha256 in
archive/manifest.csv). Licences active in 2025 by category; licensees who submitted at least one game report; reports
received; specimens reported. Live-capturing of birds (general licence) has no reporting figures in the table (N/A).
Individuals may hold several categories. The report also states 5,924 game reports from 1,265 individuals in total.

`checks.csv` is written by `tools/cc-145-report/calc.py`.
