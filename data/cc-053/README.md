# CC-053 data

- `caruana2020_table1.csv`: Table 1 of Caruana et al. (2020), "A photometric mapping of the night sky brightness of the
  Maltese islands", Journal of Environmental Management 261:110196, doi:10.1016/j.jenvman.2020.110196, transcribed
  from the arXiv version (arXiv:2002.04435, accepted manuscript), read 5 Oct 2026. Percentage of land area in each
  Bortle class, from Sky Quality Meter readings in 347 one-km cells.
- `checks.csv`: outputs of tools/cc-053-report/calc.py. Island areas come from docs/data/geo.json (OpenStreetMap).
