# data/cc-153

- `infringement_list.csv`: the 19 Member States and case numbers in the Commission's March 2026 infringements item (Representation in Malta page, https://malta.representation.ec.europa.eu/news/march-infringements-package-2026-03-12_en, dated 12 Mar 2026, read 3 Nov 2026).
- `commission_nbrp_page.csv`: every entry on the Commission's "National Building Renovation Plans" page (https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-efficiency/energy-performance-buildings/national-building-renovation-plans_en, read in full 3 Nov 2026) with the date printed beside the draft plan (the page does not say whether it is the submission or the translation date). Malta has no entry.
- `checks.csv`: figures used in the report, written by `tools/cc-153-report/calc.py`.
