Labour Party, "Int Malta: Manifest Elettorali 2026", section "Il-kaċċa u l-insib" (hunting and trapping), items 63-64, PDF page 173 (printed page 171).
Source: https://partitlaburista.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Manifest_Elettorali_INT_MALTA_2026.pdf (retrieved 5 Oct 2026; PDF of 9,042 text lines searched for kaċċa, insib, nassaba, FKNK, multa, penalti, liċenzja).

63  "Gvern Laburista biss huwa garanzija li pajjiżna jkompli jiddefendi d-derogi permessi mid-direttivi tal-Unjoni Ewropea u jiġu ssalvagwardjati l-prattiċi tal-kaċċa u l-insib. Fl-istess waqt se naħdmu mal-komunità tal-kaċċaturi u n-nassaba fuq proġetti ta' restawr ambjentali li jappoġġjaw speċi selvaġġi lokali."
    (English, our translation: Only a Labour government is a guarantee that the country will keep defending the derogations the EU directives permit and that hunting and trapping practices will be safeguarded. At the same time we will work with the hunting and trapping community on environmental restoration projects supporting local wild species.)
64  "Nemmnu li l-ġenerazzjonijiet futuri għandhom ikollhom l-opportunità li jkomplu jipprattikaw attivitajiet rurali b'mod responsabbli u sostenibbli. Se nsaħħu l-proġett tat-tnissil u t-tluq tal-gamiem b'appoġġ dirett lil dawk li jieħdu sehem f'din l-inizjattiva."
    (English: We believe future generations should be able to keep practising rural activities responsibly and sustainably. We will strengthen the pigeon breeding and release project with direct support for participants.)

No item on fines, penalties, licence revocation or reapplication appears anywhere in the manifesto text.
