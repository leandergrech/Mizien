# CC-138 data

`park_instruments.csv`: what each park instrument made under the Environment Protection Act (Cap. 549) provides,
read from the full texts on legislation.mt on 17 Nov 2026:

- White Rocks: L.N. 209 of 2026, Establishment of the White Rocks National Park Regulations (4 pp.).
- Ċirkewwa: L.N. 51 of 2026, Establishment of the Ċirkewwa Marine Park Regulations (2 pp.).
- Majjistral: S.L. 549.48, Establishment of the Majjistral, Nature and History Park Regulations (L.N. 251 of 2007, as
  amended to L.N. 184 of 2025), with S.L. 549.83, Majjistral Nature and History Park Site Regulations (L.N. 217 of
  2013, as amended to L.N. 134 of 2021).

"yes" means the text contains the provision; "no" means we found none in the full text. The `_ref` columns give the
regulation. `checks.csv` is written by `tools/cc-138-report/calc.py`.
