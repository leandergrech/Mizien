# CC-016 data

- `cruise_calls.csv`: cruise calls and passengers at Valletta Cruise Port. 2024 and 2025 from the port's own 2025
  results release (read 3 Oct 2026); 2019, 2022 and 2023 as quoted by Amphora Media (second-hand).
- `ops_uptake.csv`: onshore power supply (OPS) use, 10 Jul 2024 to 10 Jul 2025, from Amphora Media's analysis of the
  Valletta Cruise Port schedule and Transport Malta connection records obtained by FOI (17 Oct 2025; second-hand: the
  FOI reply itself is not public). Project specifications from Infrastructure Malta and the July 2024 press conference.
- `checks.csv`: written by `tools/cc-016-report/calc.py`.
