# CC-107 data

- `gozo_net_zero_arithmetic.csv`: written by `tools/cc-107-report/gozo_calc.py` (moved from data/cc-011/ on
  5 Oct 2026). Forest area needed to offset Gozo's emissions by afforestation alone: population-based estimates
  (Eurostat 2025 population 41,253 times 2.5 / 3.81 / 5.0 t per person) and the lowest and highest years of the
  2023 Energy Baseline Scenario for Gozo (Table 16), at a measured semi-arid rate (Grünzweig et al. 2007,
  3.62 t CO2/ha/yr) and an optimistic 10 t. Gozo area 67 km2 (European Commission, Clean energy for EU islands).
