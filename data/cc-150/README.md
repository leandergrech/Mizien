# CC-150 data

`nso_municipal_waste.csv`: Malta municipal waste, tonnes, 2019-2024, from the NSO news release *Municipal Waste: 2024*
(NR 225/2025), Tables 1 (generation by waste type), 2 (by collection type) and 4 (treatment by category). The xlsx
refuses scripts; it was read in the built-in browser on 17 Nov 2026 and values were copied to one decimal place.
2023 figures are revised in that release. 2025 is not yet published.

`checks.csv`: written by `tools/cc-150-report/calc.py`.
