# CC-105 data (retrieved 5 October 2026)

- `airport_exposure.csv`: people living in dwellings exposed to modelled aircraft noise from Malta International Airport, round 3 (base year 2016). Typed from ERA/Acustica, "Malta International Airport Noise Modelling, Final Report", doc. 593-14-2/3, March 2019, Table 9.2 (https://era.org.mt/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/593-14-2v3-1_Malta-International-Airport-R3-Final-Report.pdf, read from the Internet Archive copy because era.org.mt returns 403 to scripts).
- `birzebbuga_monitoring.csv`: ranges of average noise levels at five monitoring points in Birżebbuġa, from Falzon, Dalli Gonzi, Camilleri and Grima (2022), doi:10.18280/ijsdp.170732 (open access; read in full). Ranges are as printed in the text; the paper's figures were not digitised.
- `checks.csv` (written by tools/cc-105-report/calc.py): sums of the exposure table, and a full-text search of the ERA documents for "Freeport", "Birżebbuġa" and "Kalafrana". Documents searched (Internet Archive copies, 5 Oct 2026): Round 4 final report (Lemitor 2024), Noise Action Plan Malta Agglomeration 2019-2024, Noise Action Plan Major Roads 2019-2024, Annex I consultation responses, MIA round 3 report. The search runs on text files extracted with pdftotext; re-extract with `pdftotext -layout` from the URLs in literature/CC-105/README.md.
