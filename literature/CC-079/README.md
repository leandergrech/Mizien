# CC-079: Waste-to-energy: 4.5% of energy

**Status:** checked (report v1.0, 10 Oct 2026). Verdict: Misleading (moderate confidence); pending right of reply.

## Primary source (the claim)

- WasteServ, ECOHIVE news, "Malta's Waste-to-Energy Project Procurement Reaches its Final Stage", 26 June 2023
  (https://www.ecohive.com.mt/en/article?id=872332ec-b93b-4e6d-8746-732b1c175bb5), read in full on 10 Oct 2026:
  192,000 tonnes and "around 4.5% of Malta's total energy needs".
- Earlier wording: WasteServ releases of 23 Oct 2020 and 2 Nov 2020 ("4.5% as green energy to Malta's total energy
  demand"). Project page (undated): 126 GWh a year to the grid; 40% of non-recyclable waste.

## Routes tried for the wording

Claim record URL (Amphora, read; it locates the figures but does not contain 192,000); Wayback (unreachable);

and pq.gov.mt (403); TVM News (no item found). Details and dates in notes.md.

## Evidence

- Eurostat energy balances, electricity and waste data: data/cc-079/ (fetch.py), calculations in
  tools/cc-079-report/calc.py, outputs in data/cc-079/checks.csv.
