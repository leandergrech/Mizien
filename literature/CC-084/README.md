# CC-084: Tourists tripled in 15 years

**Status:** BLOCKED on verbatim wording (5 Oct 2026). No report built.

## What the record says
"ADPD chairperson Sandra Gauci (2026): Malta welcomed over four million tourists in 2025, triple the number of 15 years ago."
The cited locator (Lovin Malta, 25 May 2026, Marsaskala/Jerma Palace press conference) contains neither figure.

## What was found
- ADPD press release, 14 Feb 2026 (Maltese, "4,000,000! L-effetti tat-turiżmu eċċessiv f'Malta huma ċarissimi"), https://adpd.mt/4000000-l-effetti-tat-turizmu-eccessiv-fmalta-huma-carissimi/ . Gauci is quoted: "4 miljun turist fis-sena wisq" (4 million tourists a year is too many). The release has **no** tripling or 2010 comparison.
- Newsbook, 14 Feb 2026, https://newsbook.com.mt/en/the-impact-of-overtourism-in-malta-is-clear-adpd/ : the outlet's own sentence (not in quotation marks) says NSO's estimated 4,022,310 tourists in 2025 were "three times as much" as the 1,338,841 of 2010. It is not attributed in quotation marks to Gauci or ADPD, so it is the outlet's paraphrase or context.
- Scan of every ADPD WordPress post from 1 Feb 2026 (data/cc-084/scan_adpd.py): no "tripled" / "three times" / 2010 comparison.

## Routes tried
Claim-record URL (read in full), ADPD wp-json search and full post scan, Newsbook, MaltaToday and Malta Post snippets via web search, other ADPD tourism releases (Aug and Jul 2026 pieces mention only "over 4 million").

## Needed from the maintainer
Any ADPD statement (English release, social post, press-conference video, interview) in which Gauci or ADPD says in their own words that tourist numbers tripled / are three times 2010's. Otherwise the claim as recorded cannot be attributed verbatim. Alternative: re-scope the claim to the Newsbook sentence ("Newsbook, 14 Feb 2026") or to Gauci's quoted "4 million tourists a year are too many" (a value judgement, not a factual claim).

## Data leads (not yet tested, do not cite as evidence)
NSO inbound tourism series (annual tourist arrivals 2010 and 2025; NSO news release of 12 Feb 2026), Eurostat tour_occ / NSO. Test: 4,022,310 / 1,338,841 = 3.00. Distinguish inbound tourists from cruise passengers and from airport passenger movements.
