# CC-098: primary wording

Source: Moviment Graffitti, "VIDEO: Public officials enforce illegitimate private interests at the Blue Lagoon",
press release, 24 Jun 2026 (published on the NGO's own site, English and Maltese), read in full 15 Oct 2026 through the
site's public WordPress API: https://movimentgraffitti.org/2026/06/24/video-public-officials-enforce-illegitimate-private-interests-at-the-blue-lagoon/

Passages quoted in the report (verbatim, English text of the release):

- "The incident, which took place on Sunday, 14 June 2026, shows Malta Tourism Authority officials forcing a group of
  friends, who were lying on a rocky part of the public Blue Lagoon beach, to move away."
- "Although there were no sunbeds or umbrellas in the area where they were sitting, authority officials ordered them to
  leave and even threatened to call the police."
- "While there are concessions for the placement of sunbeds and umbrellas in certain parts of the Blue Lagoon beach,
  these in no way grant sunbed operators a permanent right of occupation, let alone the authority to use public
  officials to remove beachgoers from a public beach."
- "Moviment Graffitti is in no way calling for disciplinary action against the officials involved."

The release links the video itself (not viewed by us). The claim record's locator, The Shift (24 Jun 2026), is the outlet's account of the release.
