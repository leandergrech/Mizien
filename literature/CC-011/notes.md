## Our own analyses (data/cc-011/)

- `green_access_results.csv`: share of residents within 300 m, 615 m and 800 m of OSM green/open spaces under
  six definitions (WorldPop 2025 100 m grid; OSM snapshot 2 Oct 2026). Screening estimate, straight-line
  distances; OSM completeness in Malta is not validated.
- `gozo_net_zero_arithmetic.csv`: forest area needed to offset Gozo's estimated emissions.

## Gaps

- No official greenhouse-gas inventory for Gozo; emissions estimated from national per-capita figures.
- No Malta-specific afforestation sequestration measurements found; Yatir (Israel, about 300 mm rain a year per Yosef et al. 2018) used as
  the measured analogue. Maltese soils are thinner; rainfall is higher.
- The news reports (MaltaToday, May 2026) paraphrase the PN pledge as "net-zero through afforestation"; the
  programme lists afforestation as one of several measures.
