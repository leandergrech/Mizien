# CC-051 primary wording

## Environment and Resources Authority, "Marine Protected Areas" (topic page), read in a browser 4 Oct 2026

https://era.org.mt/topic/marine-protected-areas-2/

> "Malta's marine Natura 2000 network encompasses 18 sites and covers over 4100 km2, equivalent to more than 35% of
> Malta's Fisheries Management Zone"

ERA names its reference area (the 25-nm Fisheries Management Zone).

## Environment Minister Miriam Dalli, statement at an international event, 2025 (as quoted by Amphora Media)

Amphora Media, "FATTI: Does Malta protect about one-third of its seas?", August 2025,
https://www.amphora.media/2025/08/fatti-malta-protect-sea-marine-environment (read in full 4 Oct 2026; page sha256
4e0711fa...0380f). The minister's own text was not found.

> "Malta protects more than 30% of its maritime zone, through Natura 2000 sites, scientific monitoring, and a €2 million
> conservation programme."

Also quoted there: ERA (2021) "designated over 35% of Malta's waters as Marine Protected Areas"; Parliamentary Secretary
Alicia Bugeja Said (June 2025) cited the over-30% figure "of the Fisheries Management Zone".

## EU Biodiversity Information System for Europe (BISE), Malta country page

https://biodiversity.europa.eu/countries/malta: Malta has "a marine area of over 75,000km²"; its marine protected-area
coverage is below the EU value of 13.7% (2023). An EEA spokesperson told Amphora the area Malta reports is 75,715 km2.
