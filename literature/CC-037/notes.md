## Gaps

- No peer-reviewed study comparing Eurostat's perception indicator with measured air quality in Malta was found.
  The Aguilera abstract is from Spain and is context for the "perception is not measurement" caveat only.
- Eurostat does not publish ilc_mddw02 for 2021 and 2022 (the survey module runs periodically); the 2023 value is the latest.
- Measured concentrations (EEA air quality, Malta stations) were not compared in this check; the claim is about reports, not measured exposure.
- Amphora does not give a table code or URL for its Eurostat figure; we identified ilc_mddw02 as the series that reproduces it.
