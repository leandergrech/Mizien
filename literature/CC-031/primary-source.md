# CC-031: primary wording (supplied 5 October 2026)

**Source:** Gozo Today, "Gozo climate neutrality plan enters implementation phase", 25 September 2026,
https://gozotoday.com.mt/2026/09/25/gozo-climate-neutrality-plan-enters-implementation-phase/ (read with a
script on 5 October 2026). The memorandum of understanding between the Gozo Regional Development Authority and the
Climate Action Authority was signed at the "Gozo Towards Climate Neutrality" conference, where the plan was
presented.

Clint Camilleri's quoted words:

> "The plan published today is an important step to continue to realise our vision of Gozo becoming the first
> climate-neutral region in Malta," Camilleri said.

The outlet's paraphrase (not in quotation marks; label it as such):

> Camilleri said Gozo had already begun its transition through measures including a fully electric public
> transport fleet, investment in renewable energy and cleaner mobility, environmental rehabilitation projects and
> investment in water resilience.

**Maintainer decision (5 October 2026):** the claim is reworded to what was said. "On course" was the intake's
wording, not Camilleri's. Check:
1. The "vision" of Gozo as Malta's first climate-neutral region: what the plan sets (target year, baseline,
   scope) and whether it is measurable. If the plan sets a target, treat it as a pledge (label from the Pledges
   section of `methodology/verdict-scale.md`); without a target it is "Not measurable".
2. The reported "fully electric public transport fleet": test against Transport Malta and Malta Public Transport
   (TVM News, 28 May 2026, "Public transport in Gozo is now being completely operated with electric buses",
   https://tvmnews.mt/en/news/public-transport-in-gozo-is-now-being-completely-operated-with-electric-buses/).
