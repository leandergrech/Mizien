# CC-010: Tree-planting counts

**Status (3 October 2026):** primary wording located. The 2024 Project Green release reports more than 8,000 trees and 25,000 shrubs planted by government entities. Pledge 305 in the 2022 Labour manifesto commits to planting 100,000 trees in the next five years (PDF page 91; printed page 89). A February 2026 parliamentary answer says around 60,000 trees and more than 100,000 shrubs had been planted by government entities collectively through the end of 2025. These are separate categories and are not survival counts. The pledge's five-year window had not elapsed by the 2026 answer.

## To collect

Clarify the pledge's exact start/end dates and whether the cumulative count covers the same agencies and projects. Seek a tree-only inventory with locations, species, dates, maintenance, replacement and survival follow-up. MapMyTree and satellite canopy are separate measures, not substitutes for planted-tree records.
