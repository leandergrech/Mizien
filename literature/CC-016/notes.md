## Gaps

- The Transport Malta FOI records are known only through Amphora Media; ask Transport Malta for the connection log.
- No before/after air-quality study for the Grand Harbour was found. ERA ran a mobile station in Senglea until
  October 2024 to study shipping impact; the results were not found published.
- Basis of the 90% (corrected 5 Oct 2026): Infrastructure Malta's 2020-2023 pages scope it to the pollution cruise
  liners and Ro-Ro ships produce, with per-liner cuts by pollutant (see primary-source.md). The study behind those
  percentages (and behind the 17,000 families and EUR 375 million figures) is not cited and was not found.
- Cost: EUR 33m (launch, as reported by TVM), EUR 37m (phase 1 budget, IM 2020), EUR 49.9m (whole project, IM
  2020-2022). Which scope the EUR 33m covers is not stated.
- Electricity source: the shore supply comes from the national grid (gas-fired Delimara plants and the Sicily
  interconnector); emissions move to the power station and abroad rather than vanishing. Not quantified here.
