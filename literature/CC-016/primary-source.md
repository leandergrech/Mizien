## Inauguration press conference, 10 July 2024 (as reported by TVM, the public broadcaster)

https://tvmnews.mt/en/news/shore-to-ship-project-in-grand-harbour-completed-to-cut-down-emissions-by-90/
(Wayback: http://web.archive.org/web/20250720233339/ of the same URL)

Reported speech, not a transcript: the project "has been completed, which means a drastic reduction of 90% of the
pollution generated by ships entering the Grand Harbour"; the Prime Minister said 17,000 families around the
harbour "will directly benefit". Cost given as EUR 33 million; up to five liners, 64 MVA.

MaltaToday (10 Jul 2024) reports the 90% figure as applying when five liners use the system at once.

## Transport Malta, quoted by Amphora Media (17 Oct 2025)

> "connecting to the onshore power, at this stage, remains the prerogative of the shipping line."

## Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 (FuelEU Maritime), Article 6(1), read on EUR-Lex

From 1 January 2030, passenger and container ships moored at the quayside in ports covered by Article 9 of
Regulation (EU) 2023/1804 "shall connect to OPS and use it for all its electrical power demand at berth".
