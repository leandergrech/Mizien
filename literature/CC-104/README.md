# CC-104: PN: Malta fails EU noise law

**Status:** research started 5 Oct 2026; waiting on the petition text (verbatim wording) from the maintainer.

## The claim and its sources

- Petition No 1150/2024 (Albert Buttigieg MP and Peter Agius MEP) to the European Parliament. The EP portal shows
  only the secretariat's summary: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/petitions/en/petition/content/html?petitionNumber=1150/2024
  (the "notice to members", PETI-CM-778413_EN.pdf, downloaded empty).
- Newsbook, 12 Oct 2024 (locator): paraphrases the petition ("failed to implement ... noise mapping plans, national
  action plans and their effective implementation"; "Draft Noise Action Plan for Malta agglomeration 2019-2024") and
  quotes only general phrases ("shrug off responsibility ...", "deliberately and constantly failing to deliver any
  way forward").
- EPP Group release, 20 Feb 2025 (petition hearing): Agius quoted: "I will not accept that European rights are not
  applied in Malta ... The government cannot continue ignoring these rights." The release says, in its own words, that
  he called it unacceptable that the Noise Action Plan for Malta Agglomeration 2019-2024 "has still not been
  implemented". https://www.eppgroup.eu/what-we-do/with-eu-countries/malta/commission-investigation-on-noise-pollution-in-malta

## Evidence already gathered (for the report)

- Hjerp & Coffey (Feb 2026), study for the EP Committee on Petitions, PE 783.089, doi:10.2861/6278624 (read in full;
  see CC-020): no END infringement against Malta 2015-2025; two meaningful transposition gaps (Art. 8(3) public
  participation criteria; Annex V limit values); strategic noise maps done (round 3, 2018, base year 2016; round 4,
  base year 2021); Action Plan 2019-2024 published only in 2023; the round-4 Action Plan not published by the
  18 Jan 2025 deadline; entertainment and construction noise fall outside the directive.
- CC-020 (Supported/Largely supported) covers the same study.
