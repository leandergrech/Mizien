# CC-145: primary wording (read in a browser, 16 November 2026)

**Source:** Spoorthy Raman, "Hunters reportedly kill hundreds of migratory birds stranded by thunderstorm in Malta",
*Mongabay*, 5 October 2026,
https://news.mongabay.com/short-article/2026/10/hunters-reportedly-kill-hundreds-of-migratory-birds-stranded-by-thunderstorm-in-malta/
(data/sources.csv ref 971). Read in full in a headless Chromium browser in the cloud session on 16 Nov 2026 (HTTP 200;
curl and WebFetch had been refused). The quotations are from Antonia Micallef, public engagement executive at BirdLife
Malta, who "told Mongabay by email".

Words inside quotation marks in the article (BirdLife Malta's own wording, via Mongabay):

> "Over 10,000 hunters [are] active at any one time," Micallef said.

(The square brackets are Mongabay's. The preceding sentence, "Malta has the highest density of hunters in all of
Europe", is the outlet's own and is CC-144.)

> "Between 14 and 21 September alone, BirdLife Malta collected 21 protected birds with confirmed shooting injuries,"
> Micallef said.

Other quoted context: the turtle-dove quota of 500 was reached soon after the storm and the season closed on 14 Sep;
"Despite the official closure, BirdLife Malta documented ongoing hunting of Turtle-doves in the hours and days that
followed"; enforcement: "environmental police resources were extremely limited compared with the number of hunters
active across the islands" (quoted as Micallef's words).

Notes for the check:
- "21 protected birds ... collected" is a count of birds BirdLife itself recovered with confirmed shooting injuries in
  14-21 Sep, not a count of birds shot. The record's "21 protected birds shot" should follow this wording.
- The 10,000 figure has no stated source or licence type. The WBRU *Game Reporting Data report 2025*
  (wbru.gov.mt, ref 1083) is still refused to this environment (Cloudflare block, 16 Nov 2026): use earlier readable
  WBRU or FKNK figures, or mark the denominator unverified.
- BirdLife's own releases of 14, 15, 16, 24 and 29 Sep 2026 (read by worker C on 31 Oct) do not carry the 21 count.
