# CC-022 primary wording

## Ministry for the Environment, Energy and Public Cleanliness, press release PR250746en, 6 May 2025

https://www.gov.mt/en/Government/DOI/Press%20Releases/Pages/2025/05/06/pr250746en.aspx (read in a browser 4 Oct 2026;
gov.mt blocks automated download)

Title: "Electricity prices in the EU: Maltese households with lowest burden".

> "Malta has the lowest electricity prices for domestic consumers in the European Union when measured in terms of
> purchasing power standards (PPS). This is according to official figures for the second half of 2024."

> "While Malta has the third-lowest nominal electricity price for domestic consumers in the EU at €0.131 per unit, when
> adjusted for PPS ... the burden on Maltese families is the lowest in the EU, at 14.33 PPS per 100kWh."

Comparisons given: Czech Republic 41.00 PPS, Cyprus 35.70 PPS, Germany 35.23 PPS. Minister Miriam Dalli: "This is clear
confirmation that our policy is working for the benefit of the people." Closing line: while many EU countries saw sharp
increases compared to 2020, "prices in Malta remained stable". The release does not mention the cost of the tariff
freeze to public finances.
