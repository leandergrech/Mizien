Notes for the check:
- The claim record's wording ("11.5% ... in the EU-reported target; 25% cited in summaries") is the intake's
  paraphrase. The plan itself says 11.5% was the earlier contribution, 25% the new ambition, and 24.5% the
  projected 2030 share. Quote the plan, not the summary.
- The plan states 25% in its summary and 24.5% in its projection; report both as written.
- Test against Eurostat's SHARES data (`nrg_ind_ren`) for Malta's renewable share to date, and the Commission's
  assessment of the final updated NECP.
