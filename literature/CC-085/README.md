# CC-085: 4.7 million tourists needed

**Status:** report v1.0 and flyer drafted (10 Oct 2026). Verdict Largely supported (moderate confidence); no right of reply needed.

The speaker is the Malta Hotels and Restaurants Association. Its president, Tony Zahra, wrote in *Horeca Malta* (23 Dec 2022) that the
MHRA's Deloitte report "concluded that Malta will need to attract 4.7 million tourists each spending an average of 7 nights in Malta" to
"reach 80 percent occupancy throughout the year", and said much the same to *The Malta Independent on Sunday* in November 2024. The
study itself (July 2022) and its launch slides were read in full: 4.7 million is its middle airport-table scenario (4,680,509, p. 66),
at 2019 occupancy, not 80%. See `notes.md`.
