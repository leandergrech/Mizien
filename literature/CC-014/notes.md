## Definitions that change across years

- "Notices": MEPA and PA 2018-2023 report "stop and enforcement notices"; the 2017 report gives "enforcement notices"
  (192 + 123). 2023's 233 includes the 185 notices from complaints and excludes 27 breach-of-notice notices. 2018's
  219 excludes 310 construction-site stop notices.
- "Complaints": 2017 "registered enforcement complaints" (rounded); 2018 "planning complaints made by the public",
  with 1,572 construction-site complaints separate; 2019 includes complaints under the Avoidance of Damage to Third
  Party Property Regulations; 2020-2023 "complaints from various sources, alleging illegal development"; about half
  anonymous in 2022-2023; anonymous reports stopped in 2024 (MaltaToday).
- "Confirmed" share: 2018 "approximately half of the complaints received"; 2019 "51% of the complaints received" but
  the outcomes (737 + 824 + 141 = 1,702) match 51% of the 3,340 closed; 2020 "55% of the cases"; 2021 59%; 2022 48% and
  2023 circa 58% "of the total cases registered"; 2024 "circa half". The 2023 outcomes sum to 1,190 (48% of complaints)
  against a stated 58%. calc.py uses stated share x complaints received; the report says confirmed cases are
  approximate.
- Removals: counts given for 2019, 2023, 2024; percentages only for 2020 (45%, base read as confirmed cases), 2021
  (47%) and 2022 (38%); "the rest of the cases" for 2018. Derived counts are hatched in Figure 2.

## Gaps

- No readable source for 2008 and 2012-2016 (MEPA 2012 and 2014 reports are scanned images; no report found for 2013,
  2015 or 2016). Notices halved between 2011 (618) and 2017 (315), so the biggest fall lies in this gap. Archive by
  hand if possible.
- PA Annual Report 2024 could not be re-read on 5 Oct 2026 (Cloudflare block on parlament.mt; not found on Issuu).
- The 2018 report's "15% fall" in notices does not match the 2017 report's 315 (it implies about 258). Not resolved.
- No independent measure of illegal building (e.g. aerial-photo change detection) was found; complaints and
  confirmed cases are proxies that depend on public reporting and on PA policy.
- No peer-reviewed study of planning enforcement in Malta was found in Crossref or OpenAlex.
- The "1,500 direct actions in 30 years" figure was not verified; notices closed by direct action were 135, 52, 27
  and 74 in 2017-2020 (about 50 a year would give 1,500 in 30 years), but there is no 30-year series.
- The 2017-2023 page images are not committed (copyright); they can be re-fetched from
  `https://image.isu.pub/DOCUMENT-ID/jpg/page_N.jpg` and checked against the SHA-256 values in
  data/cc-014/pa_annual_reports_2017_2023.csv.
