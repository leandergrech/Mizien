## Gaps

- No readable source for 2008 and 2012-2018 (MEPA 2012 and 2014 reports are scanned images; PA reports
  2013-2019 are on Issuu only). Archive these by hand if possible. Issuu page images can be fetched by script from
  `https://image.isu.pub/DOCUMENT-ID/jpg/page_N.jpg` (document id from the Issuu page's HTML); this is how the 2019
  report was read on 5 Oct 2026 (id 200611082658-b4eaf717a2b602b52dbd5e973233f6a3; printed page = image page minus
  2). The PA's Issuu account (issuu.com/planningauthority) also lists the 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2023 reports.
- Upgrade still to do: replace the 2019-2023 second-hand figures with PA primary values (e.g. 2019 notices: 229 in the
  2019 report vs 228 derived). In 2019 the PA's three outcome categories (737 + 824 + 141 = 1,702) match 51% of the
  3,340 complaints investigated and closed rather than 51% of the 3,134 received; check whether the 2024 "circa
  half" has the same base.
- 2019 vs 2020: the 2019 report gives 3,134 complaints, so 2020 (3,313) was 179 higher, not the 139 MaltaToday
  reported from the 2020 report.
- No independent measure of illegal building (e.g. aerial-photo change detection) was found; complaints and
  confirmed cases are proxies that depend on public reporting and on PA policy (anonymous reports stopped in 2024).
- No peer-reviewed study of planning enforcement in Malta was found in Crossref or OpenAlex.
- The "1,500 direct actions in 30 years" figure was not verified.
