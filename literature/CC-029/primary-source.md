# CC-029: primary wording (supplied 5 October 2026)

gov.mt and interconnectmalta.com are refused to scripts. Two readable outlets carry the Energy Ministry statement
and the minister's words.

**1. TVM News (Public Broadcasting Services), 22 July 2025,** "Three submissions presented for the first offshore
wind farm concession in Malta",
https://tvmnews.mt/en/news/three-submissions-presented-for-the-first-offshore-wind-farm-concession-in-malta/
(read 5 October 2026). Minister Miriam Dalli, direct quote:

> "What was once an exploratory initiative has now entered a structured phase"

The Energy Ministry statement as reported (not in quotation marks): the wind farm "will be located beyond Malta's
territorial waters, in an area that will be declared as part of the country's Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). With an
installed capacity of approximately 300MW, the project also includes the operation and maintenance of an offshore
substation and export cable system, which will be owned by the Government."

**2. Newsbook, July 2025,** "Three firms bid for Malta's first offshore wind farm concession",
https://newsbook.com.mt/en/three-firms-bid-for-maltas-first-offshore-wind-farm-concession/
(read 5 October 2026). Minister Dalli, direct quote: "an important step forward for the project and a clear signal
that the process has truly kicked off." Statement as reported: "The contracting authority plans to issue the next
stage of the process to qualifying candidates by early next year, with pricing information to be requested only
from those who successfully pass the preliminary qualification phase."

Leads for the check (progress since): Newsbook, "Public call for Malta's first offshore wind farm issued";
offshoreWIND.biz, 21 April 2026, "Malta Opens EUR 3.6 Million Offshore Wind Metocean Survey Tender".
