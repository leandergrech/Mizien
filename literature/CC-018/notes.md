## Gaps

- The MDA's own release was found on 5 Oct 2026 (see primary-source.md), but it was modified on 17 Jun 2026 and
  the original text was not available. The MDA-commissioned study (method of its 14.0 -> 14.5 price-to-income
  ratio) is still not seen.
- Resolved in v1.2: the IMF phrases quoted by the MDA but not in CR 26/29 come from the IMF Selected Issues Paper
  2026/022 (13 Mar 2026). Whether they were in the release as first published is unknown.
- Tested in v1.2: two-year IMF cycle (supported), "first EU economy" (contradicted: Luxembourg 2000-2002), GDP per
  head "nearly doubled" (largely supported) and "far higher than the EU average" (not substantiated). Earlier EU
  cases other than Luxembourg were not searched; the release's 4% growth and unemployment statements not tested.
- The IMF assessment is about financial stability and valuation, not affordability for renters or first-time buyers.
- No peer-reviewed literature needed: the claim is about what a report says.
