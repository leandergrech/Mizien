## Question (Maltese, verbatim)

> 29696. L-ONOR. REBEKAH BORG staqsiet lill-Ministru għall-Ambjent, l-Enerġija u l-Indafa Pubblika: Tista’ l-Ministru tgħid x’kienet il-medja annwali tal-livelli ta’ PM 2.5, kif irreġistrati mill-istazzjonijiet kollha ta’ monitoraġġ tal-Awtorità għall-Ambjent u r-Riżorsi (ERA), matul l-aħħar ħames snin, maqsuma sena b’sena?

## Minister's answer (Maltese, verbatim)

> ONOR. MIRIAM DALLI: Ninforma lill-Onor. Interpellanta li qed inpoġġi l-informazzjoni mitluba fuq il-Mejda tal-Kamra.

Translation: “I inform the Honourable Interpellant that I am laying the requested information on the Table of the House.” This is a procedural answer; it does not itself state that concentrations complied with a standard. The claim being evaluated is an interpretation of the table, not a quotation of the Minister.

All 23 exceed the WHO 2021 annual guideline of 5 µg/m³ and are below the EU annual limit of 25 µg/m³ applicable during the reported years. Fourteen are above the recast EU annual limit of 10 µg/m³ that is to be attained by 2030; that is a future-standard comparison, not a finding of non-compliance in 2020–2024.
