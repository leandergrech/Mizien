# CC-075: Seventh in the EU for cars

**Status:** Drafted (v1.0, 6 Oct 2026). Draft verdict Misleading (high confidence); pending right of reply (Lovin
Malta). See `notes.md` (sources, access, how earlier Eurostat vintages were read, search log, gaps, page hash) and
`references.bib`. Data: `data/cc-075/` (Eurostat road_eqs_carhab, road_eqs_carmot, demo_gind and reg_area3 with
Eurostat's flags; Statistics Explained revisions and chart files with checksums in `vintage_sources.csv`; chart readings
in `chart_reads.csv`; `reported_values.csv`; `checks.csv`). Report build: `tools/cc-075-report/`.

## Routes tried for the wording (6 Oct 2026)

1. The claim record's URL (lovinmalta.com): **readable** with curl and a browser User-Agent; full text read. Also read
   through the site's WordPress API (post 148257), same text and date (21 Sep 2024).
2. Wayback: the Internet Archive's availability API lists no capture of the article; web.archive.org itself reset
   every connection from this network.
3. WebSearch for the key words ("Malta passenger cars 585 per 1,000 inhabitants Eurostat seventh"): finds the same
   Lovin article; no other outlet carries the statement.
4. The sources the article names: Eurostat (dataset road_eqs_carhab through the API; its release of 17 Jan 2024; its
   Statistics Explained article "Passenger cars in the EU" and that article's revision history through the MediaWiki
   API) and a graphic by Paula Lago via The European Correspondent (not found; the embedded Instagram post needs a
   login).

Verbatim wording found on route 1. Lovin Malta is the speaker: the sentences are its own text, not quotations.

No open-access PDF is committed: the evidence is Eurostat data, pages and chart files (saved in `data/cc-075/` under
Eurostat's re-use policy). Warren and Enoch (2010) is open access, but its licence is stated inconsistently (journal page
CC BY 4.0, OpenAlex CC BY-ND, PDF a copyright line only), so it is cited by link.
