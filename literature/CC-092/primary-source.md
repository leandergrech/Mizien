# CC-092: primary wording

Found and read by Miżien on 10 October 2026 (not supplied by the maintainer). Translations are ours.

## The programme

- **Document:** *Nifs Ġdid: Programm Elettorali 2026*, Partit Nazzjonalista. Published as 16 web chapters on
  pn.org.mt (the chapter pages carry `datePublished` 2026-05-18) and as one PDF per chapter
  (`wp-content/uploads/2026/05/<n>-<chapter>.pdf`). No whole-programme PDF was found (as for the manifesto list,
  `data/manifesto_coverage.csv`).
- **Access:** read in full on 10 Oct 2026 with curl and a browser User-Agent (HTTP 200). The web page and the chapter PDF
  were compared for each item below: the wording is the same. SHA-256 of every page and PDF read is in
  `data/cc-092/pn_programme_search.csv` (the documents are not committed). The English address
  (`/en/nifsgdid/<chapter>/`) serves the same Maltese text.

## Sentences relied on (quoted exactly; translation ours)

1. **Chapter Għawdex, item 46, "Mira: Għawdex Net‑Zero Island sa 2040"** (web: https://pn.org.mt/nifsgdid/ghawdex/;
   PDF `10-Ghawdex.pdf`, p. 16 of 28):
   *Infasslu pjan ċar u realistiku biex sal‑2040, Għawdex isir Net‑Zero Island, jiġifieri l‑ewwel gżira fil‑Mediterran
   fejn kull unità ta’ emissjonijiet iġġenerata tkun imnaqqsa jew ikkumpensata b’inizjattivi konkreti u sostenibbli, bħal
   skemi għall‑installazzjonijiet ta’ panelli solari, inċentivi għall‑karozzi, taxis u vannijiet elettriċi, skemi biex
   tiżdied l‑effiċjenza enerġetika fil‑binjiet, proġetti ta’ restawr ekoloġiku fuq l‑art u fil‑baħar, afforestazzjoni u
   proġetti ta’ konservazzjoni tal‑ilma u l‑ħamrija, fost l‑oħrajn.*
   "We will draw up a clear and realistic plan so that by 2040 Gozo becomes a Net-Zero Island, that is, the first island
   in the Mediterranean where every unit of emissions generated is reduced or offset by concrete and sustainable
   initiatives, such as schemes for installing solar panels, incentives for electric cars, taxis and vans, schemes to
   increase energy efficiency in buildings, ecological restoration projects on land and at sea, afforestation and water
   and soil conservation projects, among others."
   *This is the only mention of afforestation in the Għawdex chapter.* The net-zero plan itself is checked in Claim
   Check 107.
2. **Chapter Ambjent, item 01 (featured proposal), last sentence** (https://pn.org.mt/nifsgdid/ambjent/; PDF
   `13-Ambjent.pdf`, p. 8 of 20): *Il‑pjan jinkludi wkoll aktar afforestazzjoni u spazji miftuħa kemm f’Malta kif ukoll
   f’Għawdex.* "The plan also includes more afforestation and open spaces in both Malta and Gozo." (The item promises at
   least one park per locality, with more trees, shade and natural spaces.)
3. **Chapter Ambjent, item 20, "Soluzzjonijiet ibbażati fuq in‑natura"** (PDF p. 12): *Nagħtu prijorità lil
   soluzzjonijiet naturali bħat‑tħawwil tas‑siġar, restawr tal‑widien, art permeabbli, ġonna tax‑xita, afforestazzjoni u
   protezzjoni tal‑kosta biex niġġieldu s‑sħana urbana, l‑għargħar u t‑tniġġis.* "We will give priority to natural
   solutions such as tree planting, valley restoration, permeable ground, rain gardens, afforestation and coastal
   protection to fight urban heat, flooding and pollution." (Trees here are for heat, flooding and pollution; carbon is
   not mentioned.)
4. **Chapter Ambjent, item 25, "Malta Nature Network"** (PDF p. 13): the network would join Natura 2000, the valleys,
   rubble walls, *ix‑xagħri* (garrigue), ecological corridors and rural plots in one system of protection and management.
5. **Chapter Agrikoltura u Sajd, item 31, "Ħarsien u propagazzjoni ta’ varjetajiet u razez indiġeni"** (PDF
   `14-Agrikoltura.pdf`, p. 13 of 19): conservation, registration and propagation of local plant varieties and livestock
   breeds, with *programm ta’ bank ġenetiku, nursery u appoġġ għall‑użu kummerċjali sostenibbli* ("a gene bank,
   nursery and support programme for sustainable commercial use") within three years. This is the only use of
   *indiġeni* (indigenous) in the programme; it is about farm varieties and breeds, not trees.

## Wording that is not the PN's

- "Gozo would become a net-zero island through afforestation": MaltaToday's summary (PN approves its electoral
  manifesto, May 2026; read via Wayback for Claim Check 107 on 5 Oct 2026). Not in the programme.
- "A national indigenous-tree strategy": the intake's record of MaltaToday's breakdown (142040, May 2026). The article
  returns 403 to scripts and the Internet Archive was unreachable on 10 Oct 2026; a search engine's summary of it
  (second-hand ◆) describes a nationwide afforestation strategy prioritising indigenous trees and valley restoration.
  No such strategy, and no mention of indigenous trees, was found in the 16 chapters (search log in `notes.md`).
