## The claim (verbatim)

European Commission, INF/26/1376, *July infringements package: key decisions*, Brussels, 8 July 2026, section 1
(Environment), item headed "Commission calls on Spain and Malta to ensure periodic review of water permits". The
Malta sentences, verbatim:

> "Malta has failed to put in place a registration requirement for surface water abstraction and a prior
> authorisation regime to control the abstraction of surface and groundwater. In addition, Maltese law does not ensure
> a periodic review of the controls over the abstraction of surface and groundwater having a significant impact on
> water bodies."

The same paragraph introduces the case as letters of formal notice to Spain (INFR(2026)2099) and Malta
(INFR(2026)2115) "for failing to correctly transpose the obligation to carry out periodic reviews of water permits
under the Water Framework Directive", says Member States "are required to periodically review and update these control
measures, including any permits granted", and ends: Malta and Spain "now have two months to respond"; "In the absence
of satisfactory responses, the Commission may decide to issue reasoned opinions." Identical text on the Representation
in Malta page (claim record URL). The letter itself is not published; we rate the Commission's published summary.

The claim record's title ("EU: no permits for water abstraction") is the intake's headline, not the Commission's
wording. Malta does issue permits to *drill* boreholes (S.L. 549.165); the Commission's point is about authorising
*abstraction*. Headline wording, not rated.

## Search log for the "not found" statements (6 Oct 2026)

1. **A Maltese requirement to register surface-water abstraction.** Read in full and searched for "abstract",
   "surface water", "register": S.L. 549.100, 549.164, 549.165, 549.166, 549.168, 549.172, 545.14, Cap. 549,
   Cap. 355 (consolidated texts on legislation.mt). Titles of S.L. 549.160–549.184 (the last number in use) and of
   all Legal Notices of 2024–2026 in legislation.mt's ELI sitemap (`data/cc-101/legislation_mt_legal_notices_2024_2026.csv`).
   Malta's own §3.3 report on Art. 11(3)(e) (2025) covers groundwater only; the 2nd plan (p. 373) records a lack of
   knowledge on surface-water abstraction. Not searched: every other Chapter of the Laws of Malta and local by-laws.
2. **An instrument that authorises (permits) groundwater abstraction.** Same instruments. The two regimes that issue
   anything (notification, drilling permits) state that they give no right to draw water. The 2023 Green Paper and
   the 2025 interim report describe an abstraction permit as future legislation. No Legal Notice of 2024–2026 has a
   title mentioning groundwater, abstraction, boreholes, wells or water policy (see `checks.csv`).
3. **A review mechanism in the operative instruments.** S.L. 549.164–549.166 and 549.168 read in full: no term,
   renewal, expiry or periodic review of a notified source or of its use. The general duty in S.L. 549.100 reg.
   12(3)(e) is quoted above.
4. **A Maltese response to the letter.** tvmnews.mt search ("groundwater", 3 pages; "infringement", 1 page);
   WebSearch (government response, ministry, ERA, July 2026); nothing found. The reply was due about two months after
   8 July 2026; none is published that we could find.
