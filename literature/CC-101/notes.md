## The claim (verbatim)

European Commission, INF/26/1376, *July infringements package: key decisions*, Brussels, 8 July 2026, section 1
(Environment), item headed "Commission calls on Spain and Malta to ensure periodic review of water permits". The
Malta sentences, verbatim:

> "Malta has failed to put in place a registration requirement for surface water abstraction and a prior
> authorisation regime to control the abstraction of surface and groundwater. In addition, Maltese law does not ensure
> a periodic review of the controls over the abstraction of surface and groundwater having a significant impact on
> water bodies."

The same paragraph introduces the case as letters of formal notice to Spain (INFR(2026)2099) and Malta
(INFR(2026)2115) "for failing to correctly transpose the obligation to carry out periodic reviews of water permits
under the Water Framework Directive", says Member States "are required to periodically review and update these control
measures, including any permits granted", and ends: Malta and Spain "now have two months to respond"; "In the absence
of satisfactory responses, the Commission may decide to issue reasoned opinions." Identical text on the Representation
in Malta page (claim record URL). The letter itself is not published; we rate the Commission's published summary.

The record's title was changed on 6 Oct 2026 from the intake's headline ("EU: no permits for water abstraction", not the
Commission's wording; Malta issues permits to *drill* boreholes, S.L. 549.165) to "EU: no registration or prior
authorisation of water abstraction in Malta", which condenses the Commission's first sentence. The title is not rated; the
rating applies to the Commission's words quoted above. No inference is drawn about what the unpublished letter says.

## Search log for the "not found" statements (6 Oct 2026)

1. **A Maltese requirement to register surface-water abstraction.** Read in full and searched for "abstract",
   "surface water", "register": S.L. 549.100, 549.164, 549.165, 549.166, 549.168, 549.172, 545.14, Cap. 549,
   Cap. 355 (consolidated texts on legislation.mt). Titles of S.L. 549.160–549.184 (the last number in use) and of
   all Legal Notices of 2024–2026 in legislation.mt's ELI sitemap (`data/cc-101/legislation_mt_legal_notices_2024_2026.csv`).
   Malta's own §3.3 report on Art. 11(3)(e) (2025) covers groundwater only; the 2nd plan (p. 373) records a lack of
   knowledge on surface-water abstraction. Added after review (6 Oct 2026): the full text of every Act of 2024 (nos 1–41),
   2025 (1–42) and 2026 (1–18) listed in the ELI sitemap was downloaded as text and searched for "groundwater", "abstract"
   and "borehole" (`legislation_mt_acts_2024_2026.csv`, which also gives each Act's title and text length): only Act XVII of
   2024 (Environment Protection (Amendment) Act, which moved the groundwater regulations to Cap. 549 and ERA) matches. The
   six numbers after the last listed Act in each year (2024: 42–47, 2025: 43–48, 2026: 19–24) were requested and return an
   empty page, so the site lists no later Act. The titles of all 344 subsidiary instruments under S.L. 549, 423, 545, 355 and
   427 in the sitemap were read (`legislation_mt_sl_titles.csv`); 14 mention groundwater, abstraction, boreholes, wells,
   pumps or water-controlled areas: S.L. 423.12, 423.32, 423.40, 423.45 (moved to 549.164, 549.165, 549.166, 549.168),
   423.16 (repealed), 545.2 (= S.L. 545.02, the Control of Water Pumps and Wells Order, two "water-controlled area"
   declarations and nothing else), 549.21, 549.53, 549.155 (quality and pollution instruments, read), 549.100, 549.164–549.166
   and 549.168 (read). Legal Notice numbering is complete: 1–379 (2024), 1–307 (2025), 1–257 (2026), all 943 titles
   fetched, and the live sitemap on 6 Oct 2026 lists the same URLs. Titles only were read for the other 942 Legal Notices
   (L.N. 374 of 2024 in full). Not searched: every other Chapter of the Laws of Malta and local by-laws. The statement is therefore "none found in force", not "none in force".
2. **An instrument that authorises (permits) groundwater abstraction.** Same instruments. The two regimes that issue
   anything (notification, drilling permits) state that they give no right to draw water. The 2023 Green Paper and
   the 2025 interim report describe an abstraction permit as future legislation. Of the 943 Legal Notices of
   2024–2026 (all titles read), one mentions groundwater, abstraction, boreholes, wells or water policy: L.N. 374 of
   2024 (27 Dec 2024), read in full, which adds to S.L. 549.165 reg. 6(2) an exception to the drilling moratorium for
   research boreholes by public entities with ERA's prior approval. No other water title concerns abstraction (the
   others: urban waste water treatment, utilities procurement, fees for fishermen's water and electricity, inland
   waterway working time). See `checks.csv`. For the Acts and the S.L. series see item 1. Statement used in outputs: "none
   found in force on 6 Oct 2026".
3. **A review mechanism in the operative instruments.** S.L. 549.164–549.166 and 549.168 read in full: no term,
   renewal, expiry or periodic review of a notified source or of its use. S.L. 549.165 is different for drilling permits:
   reg. 8(3)(a) lets a permit carry a "term", reg. 11 allows suspension or revocation at any time, reg. 12(2) says the
   register of permits "shall be kept under review and up to date". The general duty in S.L. 549.100 reg. 12(3)(e) is
   quoted above; reg. 12(5)(b) and 12(8) add review of permits where objectives are at risk and a six-yearly review of
   the programme of measures.
4. **A Maltese response to the letter.** tvmnews.mt search ("groundwater", 3 pages; "infringement", 1 page);
   WebSearch (government response, ministry, ERA, July 2026); nothing found. The reply was due about two months after
   8 July 2026; none is published that we could find.

5. **A current count of metered private sources.** Not found. Malta's April 2025 report says "most" registered sources
   have been metered; Malta told the Commission in 2019 that more than 3 000 private sources had been metered
   (SWD(2019) 48, p. 124 n. 65). Searched: the Green Paper (no count of metered sources), the interim report, SWD(2019) 48,
   Sapiano 2020. Not searched: ERA's annual reports and its register.
