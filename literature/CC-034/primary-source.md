# CC-034: primary wording (supplied 5 October 2026)

**Source 1 (the claim):** Environment and Resources Authority, "Air Quality Plan for Malta 2025" web page,
https://era.org.mt/air-quality-plan-for-malta-2024/ (403 to scripts), read through the Wayback Machine capture of
15 August 2025: https://web.archive.org/web/20250815211515/https://era.org.mt/air-quality-plan-for-malta-2024/
(read 5 October 2026).

> The plan outlines actions that have already yielded positive results for Malta's air quality, measures committed
> to by the Government for imminent implementation, and additional proposed measures designed to further enhance
> air quality in Malta.

The same page says the plan responds to the Msida monitoring station exceeding the allowed number of daily PM10
limit-value exceedances (assessment of 2018 and 2023 data), and that the policy was approved under Article 51 of
the Environment Protection Act (Cap. 549).

**Source 2 (the plan):** ERA, *Air Quality Plan for Malta* (approved policy), 104 pages, PDF created 21 January
2025, https://era.org.mt/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DIGITAL-Air-Quality-Plan.pdf, read through the Wayback Machine
(https://web.archive.org/web/2025id_/https://era.org.mt/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DIGITAL-Air-Quality-Plan.pdf)
on 5 October 2026. SHA-256 as downloaded: 56b99ee8c0d4ea56f7b7ffa55c2d72be991f6e13a48064e20da8fcff56cf11c4.
Executive summary, PDF page 3:

> The Air Quality Plan presents measures from other policy documents that have already contributed to
> improvements in air quality in Malta, such as the reform in the power generation sector, grants for more
> sustainable transport, free school transport, improvement of ferry landing places, a fast ferry link between
> the main islands and free public transport for all.

Notes for the check:
- The checkable statement is that these measures have "already yielded positive results" / "already contributed
  to improvements". Test it against ERA's and the EEA's monitoring data (PM10, PM2.5 and NO2, Msida and the other
  stations), and against what the plan itself shows as evidence of effect.
- The page's own reason for the plan is that Msida exceeded the PM10 daily limit: set the improvement claim beside
  that.
