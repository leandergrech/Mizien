## What PwC's figures require (calc.py)

- 38% of PwC's 636,000 = 241,680 foreign and 394,320 local residents. From 405,312 Maltese citizens (1 Jan 2025) that
  is −10,992: −1,832 a year to end-2030 (6 years) or −2,198 a year to 1 Jan 2030 (5 years).
- The remainder of the change in Maltese citizens after migration and naturalisations was −914 to −1,441 a year in
  2021–2024; the count itself never fell in 2010–2024.
- Comparisons (not forecasts): Maltese citizens at their 1 Jan 2025 count would be 63.7% of 636,000 (36.3% foreign);
  Malta-born residents at their 1 Jan 2025 count would be 61.4% (38.6% born abroad); at PwC's reported range ◆
  624,000 / 660,000 the non-Maltese share would be 35.0% / 38.6%.
- "Mix of foreign to local" as a ratio: non-Maltese per 100 Maltese citizens was already 41.7 on 1 Jan 2025, so a
  ratio of 38 would be a fall; the 38% must be a share of all residents.

## Search log (statements of absence)

- **Official projections by citizenship.** Eurostat database: the EUROPOP2025 and EUROPOP2023 national projection
  tables (`proj_25n*`, `proj_23n*`) list only age, sex and projection type (table of contents read 6 Oct 2026; proj_25np
  downloaded). NSO: nso.gov.mt refused automated access (403), so its site could not be searched; WebSearch (6 Oct
  2026, queries on NSO population projections by citizenship and the PwC/NSO figures) returned no NSO projection by
  citizenship. The CBM abstract (May 2025) says no projections of the native Maltese population existed. The
  parliamentary reply reported by Newsbook was not read. Statement in the report is limited to "a web search found no
  NSO projection by citizenship".
- **Peer-reviewed literature.** Crossref `query.bibliographic`, from 2015, top 12 results each, 6 Oct 2026: "Malta
  population projections migration"; "Malta foreign workers population immigration labour market"; "Malta
  third-country nationals demographic change". No study of Malta's foreign-population share or its projection among
  the results (general methods papers only, e.g. Cappelen et al. 2015, doi:10.1111/imre.12092, not used). OpenAlex
  was rate-limited from this network after one query.
