# CC-036 notes (16 Nov 2026)

- **Primary wording.** ERA, State of the Environment Report 2018, Chapter 2 Ambient Air, section 2.4.3 Particulates, PDF p. 15
  (maintainer's browser copy; era.org.mt refuses scripts): "A source of a coarser fraction of dust is mineral extraction and
  construction, which is considered to be more of a nuisance rather than a health-related issue." Next: quarrying "produced
  1,200t of PM10 dust annually" (citing Vella and Camilleri 2005); "A minor percentage of this dust is in the PM10 size range,
  estimated to range between 11 and 22 %." The section opens with PM's associations with respiratory and cardiovascular
  disease (citing Dockery and Pope 1994; Brook 2008). No source is cited for the "nuisance" judgement.
- **Vella & Camilleri (2005)**, Xjenza 10:47-54. Full text from OAR@UM (open repository; licence not stated, so not committed).
  Tables 2-3: below-10 um share of deposited dust 13.2-22.9% (machines) and 11.0-21.8% (access roads). Abstract: about 1,200 t
  PM10 a year from 67 quarries 0.2-2 km from homes; "possible impacts on the health of the general population and, in a more
  serious manner, that of the quarrying community". **Full text** read for these parts.
- **US EPA ISA (2019)**, Table ES-1 (PDF pp. 120-122), **full table read**: PM10-2.5 "Suggestive of, but not sufficient to
  infer" for short-term respiratory, short- and long-term cardiovascular, long-term metabolic, nervous system, cancer, and
  short- and long-term mortality; "Inadequate" for long-term respiratory and others. PM2.5 causal or likely causal for most.
- **WHO AQG (2021)**, Table 0.1 (PDF p. 19): PM10 annual AQG 15 ug/m3, 24-hour 45. **Full table read.**
- **Abstract only:** Brunekreef & Forsberg 2005 (coarse PM as strong as fine for COPD, asthma, respiratory admissions);
  Adar et al. 2014 (23 mortality + 10 admission studies; suggestive evidence, short-term); Meister et al. 2012 (Stockholm,
  +1.68% per 10 ug/m3 PM2.5-10); Stafoggia et al. 2016 (13 Southern European cities; desert PM10 +0.65%, non-desert +0.55% per
  10 ug/m3). All open access; DOIs checked in Crossref.
- **Not used:** Karanasiou et al. 2012 (Environment International 47:107-114, Sahara dust review) and Azarmi & Kumar 2016
  (Atmospheric Environment 137:62-79, demolition): abstracts not available to us; leads only.
- **Search log (absence of a Malta study).** OpenAlex, 16 Nov 2026: "construction dust health effects review", "Malta PM10
  construction quarry dust", "Vella Camilleri Malta quarry dust PM10", "Malta particulate matter source apportionment". Found
  no study of the health effects of construction or quarry dust in Malta, and no Maltese source apportionment of coarse PM
  separating construction (the 2022 indoor PM2.5 apportionment in Atmospheric Environment, doi 10.1016/j.atmosenv.2022.119093,
  covers PM2.5 only).
