Sentences relied on (quoted exactly; translation is ours):

1. Subtitle: *In-nuqqas ta’ ambizzjoni tal-Gvern fl-enerġija li tiġġedded iwassal biex pajjiżna jkompli jkaxkar wara
   l-pajjiżi l-oħra fl-UE* — "The Government's lack of ambition in renewable energy leads our country to keep trailing
   behind the other EU countries."
2. *Malta hija l-uniku Stat Membru tal-UE li minflok ma naqqas, żied l-intensità tal-emissjonijiet ta’ gassijiet serra
   mill-2013 sal-lum.* — "Malta is the only EU Member State which, instead of reducing, increased the intensity of
   greenhouse gas emissions from 2013 to date."
3. *Malta fil-fatt żiedet dawn l-emissjonijet bi 17%, meta l-medja tal-intensità ta’ dawn l-emissjonijiet fl-Istati
   Membri kollha tal-UE naqset b’34%.* — "Malta in fact increased these emissions by 17%, when the average intensity of
   these emissions in all EU Member States fell by 34%." (The first "emissions" here means the intensity: the
   same sentence gives the EU's "average intensity of these emissions". It is the same slip, emissions for intensity,
   as in item 4, which is rated Mislabelled in sub-claim C. In B we note the slip and rate the figures as intensity,
   because the +17% is Eurostat's intensity figure and the sentence makes that reading clear; in C the label changes
   the facts, since Ireland's emissions rose.)
4. *Iċ-ċifri tal-Eurostat … li ġew ippubblikati fl-aħħar jiem, juru li … pajjiżi oħra qed jiksbu suċċessi
   impressjonanti fil-ġlieda kontra t-tniġġis tal-arja, fosthom l-Estonja li naqqset l-emissjonijiet b’64%, l-Irlanda
   b’50% u l-Finlandja b’44%.* — "Eurostat's figures … published in recent days show that … other countries are
   achieving impressive successes in the fight against air pollution, among them Estonia, which reduced emissions by
   64%, Ireland by 50% and Finland by 44%."
5. The release then restates Eurostat's definition (emissions in CO₂ equivalents per unit of gross value added in
   euro; an indicator of the climate efficiency of economic production).
6. *Fi kliem sempliċi, mhux qed inniġġsu aktar sempliċiment għax l-ekonomija kibret, imma llum għal kull ewro li qed
   inpoġġu fl-ekonomija tagħna qed inniġġsu aktar milli konna qed inniġġsu fl-2013.* — "In simple words, it is not
   simply that we are polluting more because the economy grew, but today, for every euro we put into our economy, we
   are polluting more than we were polluting in 2013."
7. *Dan huwa riżultat dirett ta’ nuqqas ta’ ambizzjoni min-naħa tal-Gvern Malti fuq diversi binarji. Fosthom
   in-nuqqas ċar ta’ ambizzjoni li jżid bis-serjetà l-użu tal-enerġija li tiġġedded.* — "This is the direct result of a
   lack of ambition on the part of the Maltese Government on several fronts. Among them, the clear lack of ambition to
   seriously increase the use of renewable energy."
8. *Fl-2024 pajjiżna ġġenera biss 10.7% tal-enerġija tiegħu minn sorsi li jiġġeddu. Dan meta l-medja Ewropea hija ta’
   ftit aktar minn 25%. Malta f’dan ir-rigward tinsab fil-qiegħ tal-klassifika Ewropea, it-tielet mill-aħħar.* — "In
   2024 our country generated only 10.7% of its energy from renewable sources, when the European average is a little
   over 25%. In this respect Malta is at the bottom of the European ranking, third from last."
9. A later paragraph (the one after the renewables paragraph): *Il-Gvern Laburista qed ikompli juri apatija totali
   fejn jidħlu soluzzjonijiet innovattivi, u qed ikompli jkaxkar saqajh dwar problemi li l-poplu qed jiffaċċja kuljum,
   bħas-sigħat twal imwaħħlin fit-traffiku.* — "The Labour Government continues to show total apathy when it comes to
   innovative solutions, and continues to drag its feet on problems that the people face every day, such as the long
   hours stuck in traffic." The release does not tie traffic to the emissions-intensity figure.

## Newsbook's English report (26 January 2026)

Jurgen Balzan, "PN accuses government of climate 'apathy' as emissions intensity rises", Newsbook, 26 Jan 2026,
12:30 UTC. https://newsbook.com.mt/en/pn-accuses-government-of-climate-apathy-as-emissions-intensity-rises/ (read in
full 6 Oct 2026; Internet Archive capture 13 Feb 2026). Words inside Newsbook's quotation marks, attributed to Borg
Bonello (Newsbook's English rendering of the Maltese release):

- "Malta is the only EU Member State that, instead of reducing, has increased the intensity of its greenhouse gas
  emissions since 2013."
- "this increase is therefore even more concerning when one considers that these statistics also take into account
  the economic value generated."
- "In simple terms, it is not merely that we are polluting more because the economy has grown, but that today, for
  every euro generated in our economy, we are polluting more than we were in 2013,"
- PN attributes the trend to governmental "apathy" and a "clear lack of serious ambition to significantly increase the
  use of renewable energy." (the linking words are Newsbook's).

Newsbook's paraphrase, not in quotation marks: the EU average fell 34% while Malta's rose 17%; Estonia and Ireland
achieved reductions of 64% and 50%. Newsbook's own additions (not in the PN release): Malta "leads the EU" with an
81.6% cut in emissions per unit of GDP since 2005; a 142% overshoot of the 2030 effort-sharing target; 24.5%
renewables by 2030. (The 10.7% and "third from last" are the PN's own, item 8; Newsbook repeats them.)

## The source the release names

Eurostat, news item, 23 January 2026, "2024 EU greenhouse gas emissions: -20% since 2013"
(https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260123-1, read in full 6 Oct 2026): "The
greenhouse gas emissions intensity in the EU decreased by 34% from 2013 to 2024 … The largest reductions were recorded
in Estonia (-64%), Ireland (-50%) and Finland (-44%). Only Malta (+17%) saw its emissions intensity increase since
2013. Source dataset: env_ac_aeint_r2". The release says only "Eurostat's figures … published in recent days"; that
this item is the one is our identification (dates, figures, map and definition all match).
