# CC-125 notes

- **Wording:** Commission DG EMPL news article, 29 Jul 2026, read in full on 20 Oct 2026 (verbatim; the claim record's
  quote and the 'support renovations of social housing apartments to reduce primary energy use by 60%' and 'strong and
  lasting response' passages all appear in it). Source type: primary statement. Archived by `scripts/archive_sources.py`.
- **Primary documents (full text, 20 Oct 2026; copies under open-access/, Commission documents published for reuse):**
  Implementing Decision C(2026) 5266 (recitals, Art. 1-2), its Annexes I-IV (measures, milestones/targets and payout
  values, internal-control milestone, annual allocations) and Malta's adopted plan (156 pp.). The plan was read in the
  sections that bear on the claim (summary tables, 2.1.2 retrofit, 2.1.3 and 2.5 costs, 3.1 impact); it was not read in
  full (Sections 1.1-1.5 on energy and transport poverty only skimmed).
- **Where '60%' sits:** only in the plan, p. 34 (retrofit measure I2): 'a 60% reduction in primary energy use can
  therefore also be considered a deep renovation for the purpose of drafting and implementing the SCF Plans', 'in line
  with the Commission Guidance on the Implementation of the SCF'. We did not read that guidance (C(2025) 6732): the
  statement is the plan's account of it (second-hand). Annex II sets for I2 a count of units (141 by 2030, 236 by 2032)
  and 'an improvement reaching at least a B energy performance class', with energy performance certificates; it has
  no 60% indicator.
- **Search log (absence statements):** 'no 60% target in Annex II': Annex II (all 16 milestones/targets) and Annex I
  read in full, 20 Oct 2026; 'no pre-renovation baseline': the plan's I2 sections (pp. 34-37, 123, 127) searched for
  'baseline', 'primary energy', 'kWh', 'EPC' (text search over the whole plan, 20 Oct 2026); the plan refers to
  'average reference final energy consumption prior to renovation' (p. 126) but gives no figure. The Housing Authority's
  project documentation, which the plan cites for the 'methodology', was not available to us.
- **Second-hand:** press reports of a saving 'about 3,500 tonnes' over 2026-2032 (seen as search results only,
  Malta Business Weekly / MaltaToday / Italpress). The plan states 3.54 ktCO2 per year 'by the year 2032' (p. 127),
  a different quantity; we do not rate the press figure.
- **Internal inconsistency:** the plan gives a total energy saving of 5,212,620 kWh (pp. 30, 123) and 5,220,644 kWh
  (p. 127) for the same measures; a difference of 8,024 kWh (0.15%), immaterial to the CO2 figure.
- **Literature:** none needed for the funding arithmetic. Context: Eurostat ilc_mdes01 (Malta vs EU-27).
- **Gap:** how Malta will finance the 25% national share, and the cost of the 236-unit retrofit against the target,
  are in the plan; whether the Housing Authority has started procurement is not published that we found.
