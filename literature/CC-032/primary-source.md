# CC-032: primary wording (supplied 5 October 2026)

The Government's own press release (Department of Information, gov.mt) returns 403 to scripts and MaltaToday
returns 403. Two readable sources carry the statement. Neither puts "first of its kind in Europe" inside
quotation marks, so record it as the Government statement as reported, not as a direct quote.

**1. TVM News (Public Broadcasting Services), 21 October 2025,** "First solar flowers in Europe installed at the
Gozo Multi-Modal Hub in Ta' Xħajma",
https://tvmnews.mt/en/news/first-solar-flowers-in-europe-installed-at-the-gozo-multi-modal-hub-in-ta-xhajma/
(read 5 October 2026):

> Fifteen solar flowers were installed in the Gozo Multi-Mobal Hub in Ta' Xħajma, the first of its kind in Europe,
> as part of the continuous investment by the government in transport and clean energy infrastructure. The solar
> flowers installation was announced by Gozo and Planning Minister Clint Camilleri and Parliamentary Secretary for
> Public Works Omar Farrugia.

The minister's direct quote in the same article: "This energy will be used to supply electricity, electric buses
which offer transport between Imġarr port and the Park & Ride facility." The article gives the cost as €850,000
and the claimed yield as "up to 40% more energy when compared with traditional solar panels" (statement, not a
direct quote).

**2. European Commission, Reforms and Investments project page,** "15 solar flowers installed in Gozo for
electricity generation, the first project of its kind in Europe",
https://reforms-investments.ec.europa.eu/projects/15-solar-flowers-installed-gozo-electricity-generation-first-project-its-kind-europe-0_en
(read 5 October 2026):

> Fifteen 'solar flowers' have been installed at Ta' Xħajma in Gozo for the generation of electricity. They are the
> first of their kind in Europe.

The page names the funding (RRP C1-I5, €850,000). It is an EU page publicising a Maltese RRF project; it repeats
the Government's claim rather than testing it.

Lead for the check: the product appears to be the SmartFlower (an Austrian design) sold across Europe; test
"first of its kind in Europe" against installations elsewhere in Europe before rating it.
