## The wording (verbatim, with sources)

The minister spoke at the launch of the Pre-Budget Document 2027 in Rabat on 30 September 2026, partly in Maltese. The
English quotations below are the outlets' renderings; the Maltese originals are given where an outlet printed them.

- BusinessNow (quotation marks): “The numbers already clearly show that they are higher than when Russia invaded the
  Ukraine, and I expect those numbers to remain at the same level next year. If things worsen – If it takes longer for
  agreement on the Strait of Hormuz to be found, if there is a ban on diesel exportations, if it is a cold winter – that
  bill will continue to rise,” … “what there is in the energy sector, come what may, will remain”.
- BusinessNow, reporter's text (not a quotation): “The energy and fuel subsidies for this year are expected to cost
  Government €400 million, Finance Minister Clyde Caruana said, adding that next year he expects the cost to remain the
  same.”
- Lovin Malta (quotation marks): “In just a few months, energy prices have risen very significantly. However, in our
  country, the prices of fuel, electricity and gas have not changed,” … “Malta is the only country that has continued to
  protect families and businesses from these substantial burdens”.
- Lovin Malta, reporter's text: figures “presented by the minister”: EUR 242.5m (2023), 188.1m (2025), 391.7m (2026),
  “another roughly €400 million is projected for 2027”.
- Newsbook (Maltese original): “Dak li qed nagħmlu bħalissa se nsostnuh” (English edition: “What we are doing right now,
  we will sustain”).
- TVM News (block quote, English): “No other European country is doing what we are doing.” (Maltese: “Ħadd mill-pajjiżi
  l-oħra Ewropej mhu qed jagħmel dak li qed nagħmlu aħna.”)

The claim record's text ("about EUR 400 million ... will keep electricity and fuel prices stable through 2027") is The
Malta Post's headline framing (2 Oct 2026), not the minister's words; "through 2027" does not appear in any quotation.
The record's date (2025) was wrong: the statement is of 30 September 2026.

## Gaps

- The 2026 cost is a projection; outturn data (NSO government finance statistics, Eurostat COFOG for 2026) will come in
  2027. The 2027 figure is a forecast.
- What the minister's "energy and food subsidies" series contains (electricity compensation to Enemalta, fuel, LPG,
  cereals and feed), and on what accounting basis, is in the Pre-Budget Document, not read.
- The Commission's 2023 Malta entry (CC-113) equals the October 2022 allocation, not the later Government figures; we did
  not ask DG ENER which basis it used.
- Business energy prices other than electricity (fuel bought by firms) were not tested separately; the HICP covers
  households.
