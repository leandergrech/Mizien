## Gaps and cautions

- The projections are Malta's March 2025 submission. They start from a 2023 value (1,482.3 kt) 2.6% above the 2023
  value later fixed by the 2025 review (1,445.0 kt); rescaled to the reviewed value, 2030 is still +26% (WAM) and +38%
  (WEM) above 2005. The next submission is due in 2027.
- Approximated ESR emissions for 2025 were not in the EEA datastore on 6 Oct 2026 (the folder for the 2025
  approximated inventory holds only metadata); 2024 is the latest year used.
- Our 2021-2030 balance assumes, as the Commission does, that the ETS flexibility can be counted when needed; the
  regulation cancels it in yearly instalments (Art. 6(4)), which does not change the 2021-2030 total.
- Whether Malta will buy allocations, from whom and at what price is not public; no AEA price was found, so the cost of
  closing the gap is not estimated.
- The Commission's note does not say which of 49 and 61 belongs to which scenario; Figure 13 and SWD Table 25 do.
